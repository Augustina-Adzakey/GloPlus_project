# GloPlus: U.S. Delivery Performance Analysis
## Project Overview
A comprehensive data analytics project designed to  analyzes U.S. delivery performance for GloPlus, a worldwide Supply Chain and Logistics company, to identify factors contributing to late deliveries and recommend data-driven improvements.

Using Power BI, I explored delivery performance across branches, destination states, customers, carriers and delivery delay reasons to uncover operational weaknesses and opportunities for improvement.

## Business Problem

GloPlus faces challenges with on-time delivery performance across its U.S. operations. The business needs to identify where delays occur, which customers and operations are most affected, the factors associated with late deliveries and where improvement efforts should be prioritized. 


### Stakeholder: Supply Chain Operations Manager

## Project Objectives

* Evaluate overall on-time delivery performance
* Identify branches, states and customers with lower delivery performance
* Analyze late deliveries by carriers and operational factors
* Investigate the main reasons for delivery delays
* Recommend practical actions to improve delivery performance

## Key Findings

Branch performance: Phoenix Distribution Hub recorded 50.13% on-time delivery rate, while Denver Logistics Center recorded 58.33%.

Regional performance: The Southeast recorded a 58.76% on-time delivery rate, compared with 52.48% in the West.

Carrier performance: Eagle had a 65.59% late-delivery rate, highlighting the need for further investigation.

Customer performance: Pioneer Commercial and Summit Health Supply recorded on-time delivery rates of 32.29% and 40.74%, respectively.

Delay reasons: Carrier operational delays accounted for 1,797 late deliveries in the analysis.

## Dataset Description and Privacy Note 
The dataset is a synthetic dataset generated using artificial intelligence to simulate realistic U.S. supply chain and logistics operations. The dataset was designed for educational and portfolio purposes and does not contain real company or customer data. The dataset span from January 1, 2021 to August 31, 2026. The data intentionally contains inconsistencies, missing entries and formatting problems, making it a realistic example for practicing data cleaning, transformation and visualization workflows.

The data is fully anonymized, non‑sensitive, and intended solely for educational and analytical use, ensuring compliance with privacy standards.
